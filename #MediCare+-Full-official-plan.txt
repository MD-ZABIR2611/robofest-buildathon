# 5. STRICT DOCTOR ACCESS TO PATIENT HISTORY

This is one of the most important MediCare+ rules.

A doctor must **NOT** have permanent access to a patient's medical history.

The doctor can access the patient's relevant history ONLY when:

1. The doctor is authenticated.
2. The authenticated account is a doctor.
3. The doctor owns the appointment.
4. The appointment belongs to that patient.
5. The appointment is valid.
6. The current time is inside the authorized consultation window.

Example:

```text
Appointment:
3:00 PM → 3:30 PM

2:59 PM
ACCESS DENIED

3:00 PM
ACCESS GRANTED

3:15 PM
ACCESS GRANTED

3:30 PM
ACCESS REVOKED
```

The backend must enforce this.

The frontend must never be trusted to enforce medical-data permissions.

# 27. MEDICAL DATA SECURITY

Medical data is highly sensitive.

Rules:

* Patient can access their own medical history.
* Doctor access must be appointment-based.
* Doctor access must be time-limited.
* Unauthorized users cannot access records.
* Medical records must not be publicly accessible.
* Do not expose medical information through unnecessary logs.
* Do not expose medical information in frontend source code.

```text

```

```text

Doctor?

```

