How it works

One connected loop of care

Every part of MediCare+ exists to move information safely around a single loop — from your appointment to your prescription, and from each dose back into your history.

Patient Doctor System

Safety principle — MediCare+ stores, organizes, schedules and communicates authorized healthcare information. It never diagnoses, alters a prescription, or invents a schedule.

PatientStep 01

Book an appointment

Browse verified doctors by specialty, pick an open slot, and request a visit. The doctor's dashboard updates the moment you do.

DoctorStep 02

Consult with full context

During the visit, the doctor receives time-limited, fully logged access to your relevant medical history — and it expires when the consultation ends.

DoctorStep 03

Write the prescription

Medicine, dosage, frequency, duration and instructions — recorded precisely, with no room for ambiguity.

SystemStep 04

Process automatically

The backend validates the prescription and generates your medication schedule — no manual alarms, no transcription errors.

PatientStep 05

Get reminded on time

Notifications arrive at each scheduled dose, for exactly the prescribed period — then stop on their own when treatment ends.

SystemStep 06

Close the loop

Taken, skipped or missed doses are recorded and folded back into your history — ready for the next consultation.
